Welcome to Renta

Looking for housing in Korea? Whether you're moving for university, an exchange program, or a short-term stay, we make it easier to find reliable accommodation. Renta connects you with local contacts and support – all in English and with personal guidance.

About Us

Renta was started by a small team based in Korea who saw how difficult it was for international students to find secure housing. With language barriers, hidden fees, and confusing rental systems, many students end up stressed or even scammed.

Our goal is to change that. We offer personal help, honest advice, and access to verified local contacts. Whether you're coming to Seoul, Busan, or another city – reach out and let us help you feel at home.
