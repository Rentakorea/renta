Welcome to Renta

The Renta Project was created to make student housing more accessible for international students moving to Korea. Whether you're here for an exchange, full degree, or short program – we're here to guide and support you every step of the way.

Click "Contact" to get in touch, and we'll help you find a place that fits your needs.

About Us

Renta was founded by Chaeyeon Lee, a student at INHA University in Incheon, South Korea. With first-hand experience of the challenges international students face when navigating the Korean housing market, she recognized the need for a more transparent and supportive approach.

Renta is committed to simplifying the rental process by providing clear guidance, trustworthy resources, and localized support. Whether you're moving to Seoul, Incheon, or elsewhere in Korea, our mission is to help you feel secure, informed, and confident from day one.
